1.About this policy

This policy applies to the Shloka mobile app (“Shloka”, “the app”), published by White Cap (“we”, “us”). Shloka lets you read the Bhagavad Gita and the Hanuman Chalisa one verse at a time, with meanings, recitation, saved verses, reminders and a morning alarm.

By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2.Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We have no servers that receive your data, and the app has no sign-up, login, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

In particular, Shloka does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, files, messages or call history;
  • device identifiers or the advertising ID;
  • how you use the app (we do not track which verses you read, save or share);
  • payment information. The app is free and has no purchases.

5.Internet and third parties

All verses and meanings are built into the app, and Shloka works fully offline. It may connect to the internet only in these cases:

  • Streamed recitation. Where recorded recitation is offered, the app downloads the audio file for the verse you choose to play. Like any web request, this shows your IP address to the server that hosts the file. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Fonts. The app’s fonts are included in the app. If a font style is missing, the app may download it once from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Speech. When a verse is read aloud, the app uses the text-to-speech engine already on your phone (for example, Google’s). That engine is provided by your phone or its maker, under their privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and we use no advertising networks, analytics providers or data brokers.

6.Google Play

Shloka is distributed through Google Play. Google handles downloads and updates under Google’s Privacy Policy. As the developer, we can see summary statistics from Google, such as the number of installs by country. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with app developers in your Android settings, Google may also show us anonymous crash and performance reports. These reports do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

7.Sharing verses

When you share a verse, the app creates the verse image in its temporary storage and opens Android’s share menu. You choose the app and the person you send it to. Shloka does not see or keep any record of what you share. The temporary image is cleared by the system.

8.Backups

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include Shloka’s settings and progress in your phone’s backup to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google under its privacy policy, and we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.

9.Keeping and deleting data

Because your information stays on your phone, you control it. You can delete all of Shloka’s data at any time by clearing the app’s storage (Settings → Apps → Shloka → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

10.Security

Data stored by Shloka sits in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Any internet connection the app makes uses secure HTTPS. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but because we collect no personal data, very little can be exposed.

11.Children

Shloka is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help.

12.Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, or to object to how it is used.

Because Shloka does not collect personal data, we usually have no data to act on. You can still contact us with any question or request, and we will reply within 30 days.

13.Changes to this policy

If we change how Shloka handles information, for example by adding a new feature, we will update this page and the effective date at the top before the change takes effect. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app or in its Google Play release notes.
